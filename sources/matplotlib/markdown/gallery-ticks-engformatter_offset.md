[`matplotlib.ticker.EngFormatter`](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/ticker_api.html#matplotlib.ticker.EngFormatter "matplotlib.ticker.EngFormatter")
 is capable of computing a natural offset for your axis data, and presenting it with a standard SI prefix automatically calculated.

Below is an examples of such a plot:

![engformatter offset](https://matplotlib.org/stable/_images/sphx_glr_engformatter_offset_001_2_00x.png)
