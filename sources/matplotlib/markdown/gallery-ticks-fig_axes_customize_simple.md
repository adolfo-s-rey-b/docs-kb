Customizing figure and axes appearance[#](https://matplotlib.org/stable/gallery/ticks/fig_axes_customize_simple.html#customizing-figure-and-axes-appearance "Link to this heading")

====================================================================================================================================================================================

Customize the background, labels and ticks of a simple plot.

import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

[fig](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.figure.Figure.html#matplotlib.figure.Figure "matplotlib.figure.Figure")
 \= [plt.figure](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.pyplot.figure.html#matplotlib.pyplot.figure "matplotlib.pyplot.figure")
()
[fig.patch.set\_facecolor](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.patches.Patch.html#matplotlib.patches.Patch.set_facecolor "matplotlib.patches.Patch.set_facecolor")
('lightgoldenrodyellow')

[ax1](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.axes.Axes.html#matplotlib.axes.Axes "matplotlib.axes.Axes")
 \= [fig.add\_axes](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.figure.Figure.add_axes.html#matplotlib.figure.Figure.add_axes "matplotlib.figure.Figure.add_axes")
((0.1, 0.3, 0.4, 0.4))
[ax1.patch.set\_facecolor](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.patches.Patch.html#matplotlib.patches.Patch.set_facecolor "matplotlib.patches.Patch.set_facecolor")
('lightslategray')

[ax1.tick\_params](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.axes.Axes.tick_params.html#matplotlib.axes.Axes.tick_params "matplotlib.axes.Axes.tick_params")
(axis\='x', labelcolor\='tab:red', labelrotation\=45, labelsize\=16)
[ax1.tick\_params](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.axes.Axes.tick_params.html#matplotlib.axes.Axes.tick_params "matplotlib.axes.Axes.tick_params")
(axis\='y', color\='tab:green', size\=25, width\=3)

[plt.show](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.pyplot.show.html#matplotlib.pyplot.show "matplotlib.pyplot.show")
()
