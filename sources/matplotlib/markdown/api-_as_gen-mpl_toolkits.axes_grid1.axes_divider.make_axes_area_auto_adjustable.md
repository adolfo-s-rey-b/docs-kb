Add auto-adjustable padding around _ax_ to take its decorations (title, labels, ticks, ticklabels) into account during layout, using [`Divider.add_auto_adjustable_area`](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/mpl_toolkits.axes_grid1.axes_divider.Divider.html#mpl_toolkits.axes_grid1.axes_divider.Divider.add_auto_adjustable_area "mpl_toolkits.axes_grid1.axes_divider.Divider.add_auto_adjustable_area")
.

By default, padding is determined from the decorations of _ax_. Pass _use\_axes_ to consider the decorations of other Axes instead.
