Bar chart with individual bar colors[#](https://matplotlib.org/stable/gallery/lines_bars_and_markers/bar_colors.html#bar-chart-with-individual-bar-colors "Link to this heading")

==================================================================================================================================================================================

This is an example showing how to control bar color and legend entries using the _color_ and _label_ parameters of [`bar`](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.pyplot.bar.html#matplotlib.pyplot.bar "matplotlib.pyplot.bar")
. Note that labels with a preceding underscore won't show up in the legend.

import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

[fig](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.figure.Figure.html#matplotlib.figure.Figure "matplotlib.figure.Figure")
, [ax](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.axes.Axes.html#matplotlib.axes.Axes "matplotlib.axes.Axes")
 \= [plt.subplots](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.pyplot.subplots.html#matplotlib.pyplot.subplots "matplotlib.pyplot.subplots")
()

[fruits](https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#list "builtins.list")
 \= \['apple', 'blueberry', 'cherry', 'orange'\]
[counts](https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#list "builtins.list")
 \= \[40, 100, 30, 55\]
[bar\_labels](https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#list "builtins.list")
 \= \['red', 'blue', '\_red', 'orange'\]
[bar\_colors](https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#list "builtins.list")
 \= \['tab:red', 'tab:blue', 'tab:red', 'tab:orange'\]

[ax.bar](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.axes.Axes.bar.html#matplotlib.axes.Axes.bar "matplotlib.axes.Axes.bar")
([fruits](https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#list "builtins.list")
, [counts](https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#list "builtins.list")
, label\=[bar\_labels](https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#list "builtins.list")
, color\=[bar\_colors](https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#list "builtins.list")
)

[ax.set\_ylabel](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.axes.Axes.set_ylabel.html#matplotlib.axes.Axes.set_ylabel "matplotlib.axes.Axes.set_ylabel")
('fruit supply')
[ax.set\_title](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.axes.Axes.set_title.html#matplotlib.axes.Axes.set_title "matplotlib.axes.Axes.set_title")
('Fruit supply by kind and color')
[ax.legend](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.axes.Axes.legend.html#matplotlib.axes.Axes.legend "matplotlib.axes.Axes.legend")
(title\='Fruit color')

[plt.show](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.pyplot.show.html#matplotlib.pyplot.show "matplotlib.pyplot.show")
()
