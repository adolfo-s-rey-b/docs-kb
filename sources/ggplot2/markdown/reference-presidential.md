Terms of 12 presidents from Eisenhower to Trump
===============================================

Source: [`R/data.R`](https://github.com/tidyverse/ggplot2/blob/v4.0.3/R/data.R)

`presidential.Rd`

The names of each president, the start and end date of their term, and their party of 12 US presidents from Eisenhower to Trump. This data is in the public domain.

Usage[](https://ggplot2.tidyverse.org/reference/presidential.html#ref-usage)

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

    presidential

Format[](https://ggplot2.tidyverse.org/reference/presidential.html#format)

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

A data frame with 12 rows and 4 variables:

name

Last name of president

start

Presidency start date

end

Presidency end date

party

Party of president
