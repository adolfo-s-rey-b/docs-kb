RStudio provides Push-button deployment to Posit Connect, Shinyapps.io, and RPubs.com.

[Posit Connect](https://www.posit.co/products/enterprise/connect/)
 supports a spectrum of data products, static or dynamic, developed in R and Python: Dashboards, applications, APIs, reports, and more. Connect enables flexible permissions configurations and meets all enterprise security requirements.

[Shinyapps.io](https://www.shinyapps.io/)
 supports hosting of Shiny for R and Python.

[RPubs.com](https://rpubs.com/)
 only supports hosting of static content, such as R Markdown.

### Publishing to Connect or Shinyapps.io[](https://docs.posit.co/ide/user/2026.01.2/ide/guide/publish/connecting.html#publishing-to-connect-or-shinyapps.io)

You need to connect RStudio to the remote hosting environment (e.g., Connect, Shinyapps.io) and authorize RStudio to use your account.

To connect a publishing account to RStudio, from the main menu, click **Tools** > **Global Options...**.

![Global options menu](https://docs.posit.co/ide/user/2026.01.2/ide/guide/publish/images/connecting-1.png)

Global options menu

In the Options panel that displays, click **Publishing** > **Connect...** button to add a new account.

![The Global options menu, highlighting the Publishing tab and the Connect... button](https://docs.posit.co/ide/user/2026.01.2/ide/guide/publish/images/connecting-publishing.png)

Select the publishing destination, then follow the **Connect Account** wizard’s instructions for that particular product.

![Publishing account configuration](https://docs.posit.co/ide/user/2026.01.2/ide/guide/publish/images/connect-account.png)

Publishing account configuration

To authorize the IDE to deploy on your behalf, Log in and click **Connect**. Then, return to the IDE and click **Connect Account**.

Once you have successfully configured the connection within RStudio, you can now publish content to Connect or Shinyapps.io.

### Publishing to RPubs[](https://docs.posit.co/ide/user/2026.01.2/ide/guide/publish/connecting.html#publishing-to-rpubs)

Publishing static content, such as R Markdown, to RPubs has a slightly different publishing workflow.

Once you save and knit your R Markdown document, click **Publish**.

![Publishing R Markdown file](https://docs.posit.co/ide/user/2026.01.2/ide/guide/publish/images/rmarkdown-publish-button.png)

Publish R Markdown document

Select **RPubs** as your publishing destination.

![Publishing to RPubs](https://docs.posit.co/ide/user/2026.01.2/ide/guide/publish/images/publish-to-rpubs.png)

Publish to RPubs

Click **Publish**.

![Click Publish](https://docs.posit.co/ide/user/2026.01.2/ide/guide/publish/images/rpubs-click-publish.png)

Click Publish
