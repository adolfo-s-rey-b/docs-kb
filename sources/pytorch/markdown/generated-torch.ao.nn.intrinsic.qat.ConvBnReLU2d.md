[#](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.15/generated/torch.ao.nn.intrinsic.qat.ConvBnReLU2d.html#torch.ao.nn.intrinsic.qat.ConvBnReLU2d "Link to this definition")

A ConvBnReLU2d module is a module fused from Conv2d, BatchNorm2d and ReLU, attached with FakeQuantize modules for weight, used in quantization aware training.

We combined the interface of [`torch.nn.Conv2d`](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.15/generated/torch.nn.Conv2d.html#torch.nn.Conv2d "torch.nn.Conv2d")
 and [`torch.nn.BatchNorm2d`](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.15/generated/torch.nn.BatchNorm2d.html#torch.nn.BatchNorm2d "torch.nn.BatchNorm2d")
 and [`torch.nn.ReLU`](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.15/generated/torch.nn.ReLU.html#torch.nn.ReLU "torch.nn.ReLU")
.

Similar to torch.nn.Conv2d, with FakeQuantize modules initialized to default.
