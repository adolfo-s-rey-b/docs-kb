[GetResponse](https://www.getresponse.com/)
 is an online platform that offers email marketing software, landing page creator, webinar hosting, and much more.

**Credentials**

Refer to the [GetResponse credentials documentation](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/credentials/getresponse)
 for authentication information for this node.

**Examples and templates**

For usage examples and templates to help you get started, refer to n8n's [GetResponse Trigger integrations](https://n8n.io/integrations/getresponse-trigger/)
 page.

Events[](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/trigger-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.getresponsetrigger#events)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   Receive notifications when a customer is subscribed to a list
    
*   Receive notifications when a customer is unsubscribed from a list
    
*   Receive notifications when an email is opened
    
*   Receive notifications when an email is clicked
    
*   Receive notifications when a survey is submitted
