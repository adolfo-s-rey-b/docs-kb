[Wise](https://wise.com/)
 allows you to transfer money abroad with low-cost money transfers, receive money with international account details, and track transactions on your phone.

**Credentials**

Refer to the [Wise credentials documentation](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/credentials/wise)
 for authentication information for this node.

**Examples and templates**

For usage examples and templates to help you get started, refer to n8n's [Wise Trigger integrations](https://n8n.io/integrations/wise-trigger/)
 page.

Events[](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/trigger-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.wisetrigger#events)

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   Triggered every time a balance account is credited
    
*   Triggered every time a balance account is credited or debited
    
*   Triggered every time a transfer's list of active cases is updated
    
*   Triggered every time a transfer's status is updated
