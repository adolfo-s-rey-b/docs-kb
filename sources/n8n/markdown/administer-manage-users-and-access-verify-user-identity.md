Confirm who's signing in to your n8n instance, from two-factor auth to directory-backed single sign-on.

In this section[](https://docs.n8n.io/administer/manage-users-and-access/verify-user-identity#in-this-section)

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   [Require two-factor auth](https://docs.n8n.io/administer/manage-users-and-access/verify-user-identity/require-two-factor-auth)
    : enable 2FA for your n8n account.
    
*   [Connect LDAP](https://docs.n8n.io/administer/manage-users-and-access/verify-user-identity/connect-ldap)
    : use LDAP with n8n.
    
*   [Use SAML](https://docs.n8n.io/administer/manage-users-and-access/verify-user-identity/use-saml)
    : enable SAML SSO (single sign-on) in n8n.
    
*   [Use OIDC](https://docs.n8n.io/administer/manage-users-and-access/verify-user-identity/use-oidc)
    : enable OpenID Connect (OIDC) for single sign-on.
