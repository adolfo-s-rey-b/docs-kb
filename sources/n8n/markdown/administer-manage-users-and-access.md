User management in n8n allows you to invite people to work in your n8n instance. It includes:

*   Login and password management
    
*   Adding and removing users
    
*   Four built-in [instance roles](https://docs.n8n.io/administer/manage-users-and-access/understand-instance-roles)
    : **Owner**, **Admin**, **Member**, and **Chat user** (**Admin** and **Chat user** are available on n8n Cloud Pro, Enterprise, and self-hosted Enterprise), plus custom instance roles
    

**Privacy**

The user management feature doesn't send personal information, such as email or username, to n8n.
