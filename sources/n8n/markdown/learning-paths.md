Learn n8n through interactive courses at [n8n Academy](https://learn.n8n.io/)
. Build real workflows, earn badges, and get certificates of completion.

All courses include:

*   Hands-on workflow exercises
    
*   Section quizzes and a final exam
    
*   Badges and certificates upon completion (70% passing grade)
    
*   Practice APIs (no external accounts needed for most courses)
    

For a complete list of courses, see [n8n Academy courses](https://learn.n8n.io/courses)
. To start building right away instead, see [Build your first workflow](https://docs.n8n.io/build-your-first-workflow)
.
