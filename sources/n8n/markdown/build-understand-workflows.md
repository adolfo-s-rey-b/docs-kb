A workflow is a collection of nodes connected together to automate a process. [Create](https://docs.n8n.io/build/understand-workflows/create-and-run-workflows)
 a workflow, or use a [workflow template](https://docs.n8n.io/build/ways-of-building-workflows/use-templates)
 to help you get started. Once it's running, you can [share](https://docs.n8n.io/build/manage-workflows/share-with-others)
 it with others.

If it's your first time building a workflow, you may want to use the [quickstart guide](https://docs.n8n.io/build-your-first-workflow)
 to quickly try out n8n features.
