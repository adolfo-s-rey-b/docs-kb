Manage your n8n Cloud trial, plan, and instance.

In this section[](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/use-n8n-cloud#in-this-section)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   [Try free then choose a plan](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/use-n8n-cloud/start-your-free-trial)
    : start a free trial, then compare the Starter, Pro, and Enterprise plans.
    
*   [Use the Cloud console](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/use-n8n-cloud/use-the-cloud-console)
    : open the n8n Cloud console, where you manage your instance, plan, billing, and credits.
    
*   [Update your version](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/use-n8n-cloud/update-your-version)
    : update your n8n version on Cloud.

    : set your timezone, find your IP addresses, and manage your data.
    
*   [Gateway credits](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/use-n8n-cloud/gateway-credits)
    : use AI models and third-party services without provider accounts or API keys.
    
*   [Assistant credits](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/use-n8n-cloud/assistant-credits)
    : understand the monthly n8n Assistant allowance in your plan and buy extra credits.
    
*   [Understand concurrency](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/use-n8n-cloud/understand-concurrency)
    : how n8n Cloud handles concurrent executions.
    
*   [Download workflows](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/use-n8n-cloud/download-workflows)
    : download workflows from an n8n Cloud backup in the Cloud console.
