Administer n8n by controlling access, securing credentials, managing changes, and monitoring activity.

This section helps you run n8n securely and reliably as usage grows.

Enterprise teams often spend more time in this section. SSO, directory integration, change control, and centralized logging become more important at scale. Many features covered here, including user management basics, credential security, and operational monitoring, are also useful outside Enterprise.

### A typical administration workflow[](https://docs.n8n.io/administer#a-typical-administration-workflow)

1

### Control access[](https://docs.n8n.io/administer#control-access)

Decide who can sign in, what they can do, and how you organize work. Start with [Manage users and access](https://docs.n8n.io/administer/manage-users-and-access)
.

2

### Protect secrets[](https://docs.n8n.io/administer#protect-secrets)

Store and share credentials safely. Use [Manage credentials](https://docs.n8n.io/administer/manage-credentials)
 to reduce secret sprawl.

3

### Move changes safely[](https://docs.n8n.io/administer#move-changes-safely)

Use Git-backed workflows to promote changes between environments. See [Use source control and environments](https://docs.n8n.io/administer/use-source-control-and-environments)
.

4

### Monitor activity[](https://docs.n8n.io/administer#monitor-activity)

Track usage and send signals to your logging tools. Use [Observe and log](https://docs.n8n.io/administer/observe-and-log)
 to improve visibility.
