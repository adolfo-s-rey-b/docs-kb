You build workflows from a small set of components that you connect together on the canvas.

In this section[](https://docs.n8n.io/build/understand-workflows/workflow-components#in-this-section)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   [Work with nodes](https://docs.n8n.io/build/understand-workflows/workflow-components/work-with-nodes)
    : add, configure, and control the nodes in your workflow.
    
*   [Connect nodes together](https://docs.n8n.io/build/understand-workflows/workflow-components/connect-nodes-together)
    : link nodes together to route data through your workflow.
    
*   [Add notes and documentation](https://docs.n8n.io/build/understand-workflows/workflow-components/add-notes-and-documentation)
    : annotate your workflows using sticky notes.
    
*   [Canvas Groups](https://docs.n8n.io/build/understand-workflows/workflow-components/canvas-groups)
    : group related nodes together to keep large workflows readable.
    
*   [Find your workflow ID](https://docs.n8n.io/build/understand-workflows/workflow-components/find-your-workflow-id)
    : locate your workflow's ID.
