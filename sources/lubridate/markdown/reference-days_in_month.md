Get the number of days in the month of a date-time
==================================================

Source: [`R/accessors-month.r`](https://github.com/tidyverse/lubridate/blob/v1.9.5/R/accessors-month.r)

`days_in_month.Rd`

Date-time must be a POSIXct, POSIXlt, Date, chron, yearmon, yearqtr, zoo, zooreg, timeDate, xts, its, ti, jul, timeSeries, and fts objects.

Usage[](https://lubridate.tidyverse.org/reference/days_in_month.html#ref-usage)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    days_in_month(x)

Arguments[](https://lubridate.tidyverse.org/reference/days_in_month.html#arguments)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

x[](https://lubridate.tidyverse.org/reference/days_in_month.html#arg-x)

a date-time object

Value[](https://lubridate.tidyverse.org/reference/days_in_month.html#value)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------

An integer of the number of days in the month component of the date-time object.
