Completed construction in the US in 2018

Completed construction in the US in 2018

Usage[](https://tidyr.tidyverse.org/reference/construction.html#ref-usage)

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    construction

Format[](https://tidyr.tidyverse.org/reference/construction.html#format)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------

A dataset with variables:

Year,Month

Record date

`1 unit`, `2 to 4 units`, `5 units or mote`

Number of completed units of each size

Northeast,Midwest,South,West

Number of completed units in each region

Source[](https://tidyr.tidyverse.org/reference/construction.html#source)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Completions of "New Residential Construction" found in Table 5 at [https://www.census.gov/construction/nrc/xls/newresconst.xls](https://www.census.gov/construction/nrc/xls/newresconst.xls)
 (downloaded March 2019)
